# Content decisions and website configuration

The owner approved the supplied imagery and content and selected **RockValle Resources** as the final name on 11 October 2026. This revision applies that name to the logo, navigation, page copy, metadata, package identity and enquiry templates.

The top utility strip has been removed. Public content-note banners and draft/ownership/availability caveats have been removed across business, commodity, gallery, sustainability, governance and website-information pages. Supporting copy is rewritten as useful editorial information.

All 65 supplied JPEGs are retained, including 11 images in the latest archive. RR-56 and RR-58 are byte-identical; both supplied records are retained. The gallery has a Mineral guides category, and the new charts and specimens also appear in related commodity and exploration content. No company statistics, office addresses, project locations, certifications or leadership names were invented.

Public contact details, domain settings and SMTP credentials are configured through `.env.example`. The enquiry service reports actual readiness and records success only after an accepted mail recipient. Download enquiry creates a local file. These functional states remain accurate.

Original editorial sources include Glencore commodity, marketing, recovery and sustainability pages, with general mineral context from the USGS National Minerals Information Center. The writing is original; outside company performance data is not attributed to RockValle.
